# Emergency Information Plan

**Original PhiARK educational starter guide (2026).** This checklist is not a replacement for medical, environmental, or structural safety instructions.

## Start with the environment you actually have

Write down the people, pets, mobility requirements, regular medications, available transportation, and conditions that could make shelter unsafe. Keep a paper copy where people can find it without unlocking a computer.

## Keep essential contact and location information on paper

Record a meeting place, an out-of-area contact, emergency numbers appropriate to your location, evacuation destinations, nearby clinics, veterinary services, and alternate routes. Review and update this record regularly.

## Inventory and test, don't guess

List drinking-water storage, food with known shelf life, first-aid supplies, sanitation supplies, lighting, batteries, charging, radios, and weather-appropriate clothing. Label containers and test anything you would depend on. Plan explicitly for infants, older adults, disabilities, and animal needs.

## Manage knowledge as an emergency supply

- Keep a paper quick-reference binder for situations without power.
- Store local copies of *current* instructions for any equipment you actually own, including model numbers.
- Keep a removable backup of the PhiARK data directory and verify checksums after copying.
- Test your offline reader with the network disconnected, not just with a disconnected browser tab.
- Periodically recheck safety-critical guidance and document revision dates.

## During an emergency

Follow current local alerts and evacuation orders when you have them. Avoid using unknown water, suspicious food, damaged electrical equipment, or compromised structures based on improvisation. Get trained help for injuries or conditions beyond your skills.

**The aim is to know what you have, where it is, what is verified, and what remains unknown.**
