# Knowledge Verification Field Notes

**Original PhiARK educational starter guide (2026).**

Before making a consequential decision from a manual, answer:

1. **Origin:** Who wrote it? Are the publisher, author and edition identifiable?
2. **Time:** What date was it published or revised? Could guidance have changed?
3. **Method:** Does it explain measurements, limits, tools and assumptions clearly enough to repeat or check?
4. **Independent check:** Is there another credible source or a safe independent test supporting the method?
5. **Conditions:** Does the advice apply to your actual equipment, climate, environment and skill level?
6. **Safety:** What could go wrong? Are there clear stop conditions and safer alternatives?
7. **Permission:** Can you keep, adapt or share a copy? Record the exact license.
8. **Integrity:** Are the file bytes unmodified since you imported them? Run PhiARK's SHA-256 verifier.

Distinguish facts, theories, reports, simulations, inferences, unknowns, and personal experience. A strong claim without a checkable method remains a claim, even when confidently presented.

### Paper receipt

```text
Document title:
Publisher / author:
Exact version and date:
Original source:
Verification date:
Second source or test:
Applicable to:
Known limits:
Rights/license:
Status: candidate / checked / do not use
```

PhiARK's software verifies file bytes, **not** the truth of a document. Record the independent evidence separately.
